# RealityCheck

**See the future before you change the present.**

RealityCheck replays what really happened in a factory, lets you stop at any recorded moment, change one
decision, and simulates the other future beside the one that happened.

- **Demo video (3 minutes):** https://youtu.be/Rw4WrUri5G0
- **Code:** https://github.com/DRishu070501/RealityCheck

The factory here is a synthetic demonstration factory, *Surat Diamond Works*. You can change anything; it is
restored whenever the Space restarts. The Space sleeps after a while without visitors, and the first visit
after that takes about a minute to start.

Kiroween 2026 entry, Frankenstein category. Built spec-first with Kiro.
